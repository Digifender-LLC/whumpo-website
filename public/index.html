GOOD TO KNOW

A few things
you might wonder.

You can start with the basics and learn the rest along the way.

Do I need to know anything about computers?

Just what you’d like to accomplish. You might say “make a page for my landscaping business” or “help our club share files.” TownHello takes care of the technical choices and explains the things you need to decide.

Is this only for websites?

Today you can create websites with photos, contact forms and directories. Shared file spaces, private tools, game servers and always-on apps are future possibilities; they are not available in this preview.

Can I start small and add more later?

Absolutely. Begin with a personal page or one useful tool. When you’re ready, return to your conversation and describe the next thing you need.

Will I know what it costs?

The invite-only preview is free with a limited testing allowance. Draft launch plans start at $17/month, with annual savings and a qualified domain benefit. Your workspace shows current test usage. Paid plans and domain redemption are not available yet.

What if I already have a domain?

You can record your domain and verify ownership in the workspace. Custom-domain hosting is still being tested. For now, publish using a preview address on sites.digifender.com.
