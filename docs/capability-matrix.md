## Evidence rules

- **Source:** relevant implementation exists at the stated baseline; feature contracts below describe its bounds.
- **Configured:** a binding/schema/service was observed, not proof of valid provider consent, billing or customer operation.
- **Recorded local/live:** the linked dated feature record describes that test scope. Those journeys were not rerun for this reconciliation.
- **Device:** a desktop browser at a narrow viewport is not physical mobile/passkey/touch/keyboard acceptance. `Unknown` means no sufficient evidence was found. `N/A` applies to an exclusively backend requirement, not the complete customer journey.
- **Available:** private, owner-only testing under the current controls. Source presence or an old successful fixture does not override a newer safety hold or owner deferral.
