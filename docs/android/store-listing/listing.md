## Full description (4000 max)

Whumpo is where you run your small-business website by talking to it.

Tell Whumpo what you need in plain words: add a service, change your hours, fix a price, update a photo. Whumpo prepares the change and shows it to you first. Nothing goes live until you say so.

What you can do in the app:

- Chat. Ask for a change, answer a quick form when Whumpo needs details, and see exactly what it plans to do.
- Review and approve. Every change that goes live, restores or publishes waits for your explicit tap, with a clear receipt afterwards.
- Projects. See each site or app you own, whether it is live, and its saved versions. Open a preview in your browser.
- Activity. Follow what happened and when. If something needs your review, it is waiting there.
- Photos. Pick a photo from your phone and add it to a project. Location and camera details are removed before upload.
- Dark and light themes, large text and screen reader support.

Sign in with a passkey: no passwords to remember or leak. Your fingerprint, face or screen lock never leaves your phone.

Whumpo is invite-only while it is in private testing. New accounts, passkey recovery and billing happen on the Whumpo website; the app opens your browser for those.

Questions or trouble? Open Settings, then Help and support.
