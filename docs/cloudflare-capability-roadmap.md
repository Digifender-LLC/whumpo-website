# Cloudflare capability catalog and Whumpo roadmap

**Reviewed September 29, 2026.** Product descriptions and links below use Cloudflare's official documentation. Cloudflare availability, feature names, limits and prices change; recheck official pricing and account eligibility before implementation or launch. “Available on Free” does not mean unlimited, costless at scale, or included in Whumpo's current account entitlement.

## Whumpo today

**Whumpo status reconciled October 1, 2026 UTC:** use the [dated capability matrix](capability-matrix.md) for source/configuration/local/live/device evidence. The provider product descriptions and price notes below retain their September 29 review date; this reconciliation did not revalidate Cloudflare pricing or entitlement.

Whumpo itself runs a control/workspace Worker, a separate published-site host, a visitor-app Worker and a bounded QuickJS runtime, with Workers Static Assets, D1 control storage, private R2 and configured Turnstile/observability. The [recorded October 1 04:28 UTC release readback](releases.md#readable-comparison-recovery--october-1-2026-utc) confirms all four versions and 113 applied migrations (historical: the October 4 cutover initialized the complete current schema, 219 tables, with no numbered migrations, and source at `d948f06` on October 11 composes 221 tables; see [releases](releases.md#pr-160-shared-modules-and-contact-email-cutover--october-4-2026-utc)). [Private maintenance diagnostics](maintenance.md) now has recorded natural hourly and daily execution. This status update inspected saved evidence; it did not repeat Cloudflare reads.

Static-site generation and reviewed publication are implemented for owner testing. Uploaded profile/project photos are held pending trusted scanning; CSAM scanning is planned and owner-deferred, and no configured detector approves them. Customer Cloudflare OAuth/DNS flows exist in source and the client/vault binding names are present, while real consent/DNS/revocation acceptance remains open. The September 30 inventory found no customer credentials or live customer services and absent customer GitHub/custom-hostname activation bindings; those counts were not re-read. October 1 release evidence confirms unchanged binding names and empty CMS registrations. **CMS testing is owner-deferred**.

Whumpo does not expose a general Cloudflare account console or provision arbitrary Cloudflare services per customer. A Cloudflare OAuth grant is not permission to silently create billable resources. Workers Paid included allowance is authorized for Whumpo; any new capability still needs its specific ownership, review, cost and activation checks.

## Delivery sequence

1. **Low-friction customer value on the existing stack:** expand domain connection/setup and health diagnostics; per-site analytics/traffic and log views; deployment/version observability; email form delivery using a separately selected email provider or Cloudflare Email Routing where suitable; optional security/cache/redirect presets with explicit review.
2. **Useful platform capabilities with manageable cost:** durable background publication/media work following the [D1-first job decision](background-jobs.md), with Queues/Workflows gated on measured need; tenant-scoped D1/R2 resources for customer apps; AI Search for each site's approved content; AI Gateway for model telemetry/policy; Workers AI as an opt-in model provider after price and quality evaluation.
3. **Chargeable advanced hosting:** isolated customer Workers and bindings via Workers for Platforms; containers for compatible workloads; Durable Objects for interactive/stateful apps; custom domains/advanced routing. This requires metering, quotas, abuse controls, incident operations, and clear customer-specific billing before launch.
4. **Defer pending product-market need, entitlement, or cost case:** VPC/private networking, video/live/realtime, enterprise Zero Trust/security services, paid traffic acceleration, advanced data analytics/catalogs, and infrastructure-management surfaces. Do not build a broad dashboard that merely mirrors Cloudflare's account UI.

## Product-by-product map

Status labels: **Existing** means Whumpo itself uses it; **Partial** means a constrained integration exists; **Candidate** means feasible but not implemented; **Defer** means substantial cost, entitlement, operational, or product-fit barrier. Free-tier notes are directional, not a quote.

## Cost and launch gates

- As of September 29, 2026, Cloudflare documents a **$5/month minimum Workers Paid plan**; Workers for Platforms is **$25/month**. These are account-level costs before usage and do not include AI inference, media, premium networking, external providers or overages. See [Workers pricing](https://developers.cloudflare.com/workers/platform/pricing/) and [Workers for Platforms pricing](https://developers.cloudflare.com/cloudflare-for-platforms/workers-for-platforms/reference/pricing/).
- Whumpo's owner authorized the existing Workers Paid ($5/month) resources; GitHub remains free-only. New paid services, upgrades, purchases and customer operations require their applicable concrete review. Provider entitlement/headroom is not established by this catalog.
- Before exposing a service to customers, record exact feature entitlement, current price, usage unit, hard/soft limits, data location/retention, tenant binding strategy, deletion behavior, failure recovery, and whether Whumpo or the customer pays.
- Label paid/add-on capabilities in product UI before enabling them. “Free tier” means limited availability, never a guarantee of no overage or future pricing changes.
