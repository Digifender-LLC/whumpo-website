## Why pickers choose finished work

`ready` is a planning state, not a completion field ([readiness-reconciliation-2026-10-20.md](readiness-reconciliation-2026-10-20.md), [execution.md](execution.md)). The schema accepts only `ready`, `planned`, `blocked` and `deferred`. A task whose contract or implementation merged therefore stays `ready` forever, and the only completion signal is prose in `evidence`.
